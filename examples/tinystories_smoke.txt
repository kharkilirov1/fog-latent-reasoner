Mira found a small blue key under the old garden bench. She asked her brother Leo what it could open. Together they tried the shed, the mailbox, and a wooden box in the attic. The key opened the box, where they found their grandmother's paper stars and a kind note.
At sunrise, a young fox heard a bell near the river. The bell belonged to a lost goat, so the fox followed the sound and showed the goat a safe path home. The farmer thanked the fox with a warm blanket and a bowl of berries.
Niko built a tiny boat from a leaf and a twig. Rain filled a puddle beside his house, and the boat sailed from one end to the other. When the wind turned strong, Niko used a string to bring the boat safely back.
Lina wanted to bake bread, but the kitchen was cold. She closed the window, measured the flour, and waited while the dough grew. By evening the bread was ready, and she shared it with her neighbors.
